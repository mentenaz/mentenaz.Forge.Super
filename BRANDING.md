# Branding

This file says what is meant by the Mentenaz Forge name and artwork in this repository, how it relates to the licences of the
code, and what you may do with it.

## What this covers

- the names **Mentenaz Forge**, **Forge Super** and **Mentenaz**, and
- the logo and artwork of Mentenaz Forge, which are these files:
  - `assets/images/mentenaz_forge_super.svg`
  - `crates/gpui_component_assets/assets/icons/Forge_Cockpit2.svg`
  - `crates/zed/resources/Forge_super.png`
  - `crates/zed/resources/Neon Blue Forge Anvil Icondev.png`
  - `crates/zed/resources/Neon Green Arcane Forge IconPreview.png`
  - `crates/zed/resources/Purple Neon Forge EmblemNighly.png`
  - the Forge app icons: `crates/zed/resources/app-icon*.png` (including the `@2x` ones),
    `crates/zed/resources/windows/app-icon*.ico` and `inno/x86_64/app-icon*.ico`
  - the screenshots of the application in `docs/screenshots/`, which show the Forge interface

## How this relates to the licences

The source code in this repository is under the licences that come with it (`LICENSE-GPL`, `LICENSE-APACHE`, and the licence of
each crate). The names and the artwork listed above are **not** offered under those licences. They are in the repository so that
the application builds and so that you can see it, and the permissions below are all you get for them.

## What you may do

- Refer to the project by its name, and show the logo or a screenshot unchanged, to say what it is: in an article, a post, a
  talk, or a link to this repository.
- Build and run your own copy of the application for yourself.
- **Keep, change or remove the Forge favicon and the Forge image in a project you make with the Mentenaz Forge project
  templates.** That project is yours.

## What you may not do

- Use the name or the artwork to suggest that your fork, product or service is Mentenaz Forge, is made by it, or is endorsed
  by it.
- If you distribute a modified version of the application, replace the names and the artwork listed above with your own first
  (the code you may redistribute under its licences; the branding stays here).
